# Loom — Play Store Listing

## Short description
Your launcher that learns. The right app, at the right time.

## Full description
Loom is a minimal Android launcher that quietly learns how you use your phone.

No folders. No widgets. No clutter. Just a clean list of apps — ranked by what you're most likely to need right now, based on the time of day, day of the week, and your past habits.

**How it works**
Every time you open an app, Loom remembers. Over time it builds a picture of your routines: Spotify on your morning commute, Slack during work hours, Netflix in the evening. The ranking updates automatically — no setup, no configuration.

**Features**
• Smart ranking — apps surface when you actually use them
• Instant search — swipe down to filter by name
• Clean aesthetic — pure black UI, easy on OLED screens
• Swipe to reset — clear an app's history with a left swipe
• No internet required — everything stays on your device
• No permissions — Loom only sees what you tap

**Privacy**
Loom stores nothing but your own launch history, locally on your device. No analytics, no ads, no servers.
