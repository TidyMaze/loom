# Short Video Scripts, French versions (TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts)

Three short concepts (15 to 30 s) for the French digital-detox audience. Directions are in English; on-screen text and voice-over are in French.

---

## Concept 1: "My home screen, before and after" (15 seconds)

**Hook**: A busy phone next to Loom.
**Audio**: Calm lofi or ambient track.

- **0:00 - 0:03**: *Regular home screen*: red badges, Instagram widget, bright icons. On-screen text: *"Mon ecran d'accueil ressemblait a ca."*
- **0:03 - 0:08**: *Set Loom as default launcher*: the screen turns black with a calm grid of icons. On-screen text: *"Il y a 3 mois je suis passe a ca."*
- **0:08 - 0:12**: *Same screen at 8:00 and at 19:00*: calendar and coffee app on top in the morning, music and gym in the evening. On-screen text: *"Il remonte les applis dont j'ai besoin. J'ai rien regle."*
- **0:12 - 0:15**: *App name*: On-screen text: *"Hors-ligne. Sans pub. Gratuit sur GitHub."*

---

## Concept 2: "Stop searching for the same apps" (20 seconds)

**Hook**: Typing in search vs one tap.

- **0:00 - 0:05**: *Someone types 3 letters in the search bar and gets it wrong*: On-screen text: *"Combien de fois par jour tu cherches les 4 memes applis ?"*
- **0:05 - 0:12**: *Switch to Loom*: *"Avec Loom, l'appli que je veux est deja la premiere une fois sur trois."*
- **0:12 - 0:17**: *Swipe down*: search for less frequent apps.
- **0:17 - 0:20**: On-screen text: *"790 Ko. Open source. Lien en bio."*

---

## Concept 3: "No need for a 300 euro dumbphone" (30 seconds)

**Hook**: *"Pas besoin de payer 300 euros pour un dumbphone."*

- **0:00 - 0:07**: *"Certains achetent un telephone a 300 euros pour moins l'utiliser."*
- **0:07 - 0:15**: *Smartphone with Loom*: *"Ou alors tu changes de lanceur, ca prend 30 secondes."*
- **0:15 - 0:22**: *"Pas de permission internet, pas de pistage, un ecran noir qui menage la batterie OLED."*
- **0:22 - 0:30**: *"Gratuit et open source. Loom, sur GitHub."*
