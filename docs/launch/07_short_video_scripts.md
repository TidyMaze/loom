# Short Video Scripts (TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts)

Three short concepts (15 to 30 s) for digital minimalism and Android customization audiences.

---

## Concept 1: "My home screen, before and after" (15 seconds)

**Hook**: A busy phone next to Loom.
**Audio**: Calm lofi or ambient track.

- **0:00 - 0:03**: *Normal home screen*: red badges, Instagram widget, bright icons. Text on screen: *"My home screen used to look like this."*
- **0:03 - 0:08**: *Set Loom as default launcher*: the screen turns black with a list of app names. Text on screen: *"Three months ago I switched to this."*
- **0:08 - 0:12**: *Same screen at 8:00 and at 18:00*: calendar and coffee app on top in the morning, gym and messages in the evening. Text on screen: *"It moves the apps I need to the top. I set nothing up."*
- **0:12 - 0:15**: *App name*: Text on screen: *"Works offline. No ads. Free on GitHub."*

---

## Concept 2: "Stop searching for the same apps" (20 seconds)

**Hook**: Typing in a search bar vs one tap.

- **0:00 - 0:05**: *Someone typing in the search bar and getting it wrong*: Text on screen: *"How many times a day do you search for the same 4 apps?"*
- **0:05 - 0:12**: *Switch to Loom*: *"With Loom, the app I want is already first one time in three."*
- **0:12 - 0:17**: *Pull down*: show search for the apps that aren't near the top.
- **0:17 - 0:20**: Text on screen: *"790 KB. Open source. Link in bio."*

---

## Concept 3: "You don't need a $300 minimalist phone" (30 seconds)

**Hook**: "You don't need to buy a $300 minimalist phone."

- **0:00 - 0:07**: *Light Phone or similar on screen*: *"People pay $300 for a phone that does less."*
- **0:07 - 0:15**: *Regular Android with Loom*: *"Or you change your launcher in 30 seconds."*
- **0:15 - 0:22**: *"No internet permission, no tracking, black screen that's easy on OLED batteries."*
- **0:22 - 0:30**: *"Free and open source. Loom, on GitHub."*
