Loom is a minimal Android launcher that quietly learns how you use your phone.

No folders. No widgets. No clutter. Just a clean list of apps, ranked by what you are most likely to need right now based on time of day, day of the week, and your past habits.

FEATURES:
• Smart ranking: Apps surface automatically when you actually use them.
• Context awareness: Learns routines by time of day, day of the week, and launch transitions.
• Instant search: Swipe down anywhere to filter apps by name with fuzzy matching.
• Clean aesthetic: Pure black UI, easy on eyes and battery efficient on OLED screens.
• 100% on-device & private: No internet connection required. Zero telemetry, zero tracking.
• Fallback ready: Fully usable even with zero permissions granted.

Tested on real daily usage across 10,000+ app launches over 3 months:
• 1 in 3 launches is already ranked #1 on screen.
• Over 80% of launches require zero searching or scrolling.
• Less than 1 ms inference latency with zero battery impact.
